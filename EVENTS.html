School Events

MASQUERADE BALL

A festive event where students wear masks and costumes, often accompanied by music, dancing, and performances. It allows everyone to be creative, have fun, and enjoy a sense of mystery and excitement while celebrating school spirit and social interaction.

INTRAMURALS

A sports event where students compete and express their skills in badminton, volleyball, and other games to promote teamwork and fitness.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP WEEK

A week where students develop business ideas, sell products, and learn teamwork, creativity, and leadership skills.

NUTRITION MONTH

An event promoting healthy eating habits through contests, exhibits, and awareness activities.

COLORED FUN RUN

A school-wide running event that promotes fitness, teamwork, and community spirit, where students participate together for health and enjoyment.
